About

Vegetarian Films produces auteur-driven feature films, short films and music videos in Finland and Spain.

Our films have screened at festivals from Beijing and Goa to Gothenburg and São Paulo, been released in Finnish cinemas by Nordisk Film, SF Film and Pirkanmaan Elokuvakeskus, and broadcast by Yle. Time Out won Best Global Feature at the Oaxaca FilmFest in 2019. We also co-produce with theatres: in the Theatre Goes Movies series (2013), KOM-teatteri, Ryhmäteatteri and Viirus each made a feature film of their own.

Juha Kukkonen

Producer and actor

Plays the lead, Olli, in Don Quijote de Barcelona (2026), which he also executive-produced. Also a writer. Artistic director and CEO of Ryhmäteatteri, Helsinki, 2009–2026. Lead roles include Helsinki Syndrome (Yle 2024), Ratamo (2018) and the Jussi Award–winning Joki (2001; Jussis 2002). MFA, Theatre Academy Helsinki.

Jarmo Lampela

Founding member · director and writer

Twelve feature films and three Jussi Awards: Best Film for Sairaan kaunis maailma (Jussi 1998), Best Director and Best Screenplay for Joki (2001; Jussis 2002) and Best Director for Eila (Jussi 2004). Professor of Film Directing at Aalto University, 2007–2014. Now Head of Drama at Yle, and for that reason takes no part in Vegetarian Films' decision-making.
